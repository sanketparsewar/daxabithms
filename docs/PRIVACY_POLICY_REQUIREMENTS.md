# DaxabitHMS: Customer Privacy Policy Requirements & Template

This document provides restaurant owners and operators with the legal requirements, mandatory disclosures, and template language needed to publish a compliant **Customer Privacy Policy**, with specific alignment to **India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and international privacy frameworks.

---

## 1. Statutory Obligations for Restaurant Operators

Restaurants collecting customer personal details (mobile numbers at the billing counter, online order deliveries, or table reservations) act as **Data Fiduciaries** under privacy laws.

### Mandatory Compliance Requirements
1. **Notice & Consent at Collection Point**:
   - Customers must be clearly informed *why* their phone number or email is requested (e.g. *"To send your GST digital invoice and track loyalty points"*).
   - Providing personal data must be voluntary. A customer who refuses to share contact details **must still be permitted to purchase food and receive a standard paper receipt**.
2. **Purpose Limitation**:
   - Data collected for digital invoice delivery must not be repurposed for aggressive unsolicited marketing SMS campaigns without separate, affirmative consent.
3. **Data Retention & Statutory Interplay**:
   - **Marketing Data**: Must be deleted or anonymized upon customer request.
   - **Tax Invoicing Records**: Under Section 36 of India's CGST Act 2017, invoice ledgers and accounting records **must be retained for 72 months (6 years)**. The privacy policy must explicitly disclose this legal retention obligation.
4. **Grievance Redressal Officer**:
   - The restaurant must appoint a designated contact person (e.g. General Manager or Owner) to handle customer data correction or deletion requests.

## 2. Customer Privacy Policy Template (Ready to Adapt)

*Below is a standard template for restaurant owners to publish on their website, customer display, or digital menu:*

---

### **PRIVACY POLICY FOR GUESTS & CUSTOMERS**
**Last Updated: [Date]**
**Restaurant Name: [Insert Restaurant Trade Name]**
**Operated by: [Insert Legal Company Name]**
**Registered Address: [Insert Address]**

#### 1. Information We Collect
We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide you with dining, billing, and hospitality services:
- **Contact Details**: Name, mobile telephone number, and optional email address.
- **Order & Billing History**: Dishes ordered, table numbers, date and time of visit, total bill amount, payment method (Cash / Card / UPI reference). *We do not store complete credit/debit card numbers or CVV codes.*
- **Delivery Information**: Delivery street address, landmark, and delivery instructions (for takeaway and direct delivery orders).
- **Dietary & Allergy Notes**: Voluntary allergen disclosures provided for kitchen safety.

#### 2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:
1. Generating and delivering itemized tax receipts and digital GST invoices via SMS or email.
2. Managing your table reservations and notifying you when your dining table is ready.
3. Calculating customer loyalty reward points and applying discount vouchers.
4. Fulfilling food preparation and home delivery orders.
5. Complying with statutory tax, food safety, and accounting regulations.

#### 3. How We Protect Your Information
- Your information is stored on our secure, password-protected restaurant management servers with cryptographic encryption.
- We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing companies or brokers.
- Access is strictly restricted to authorized staff members on a need-to-know basis.

#### 4. Data Retention Period
- **Invoicing & Accounting Data**: In compliance with national tax regulations (including Section 36 of the Indian CGST Act, 2017 and local commercial laws), completed billing transactions and invoice records are preserved for **72 months (6 years)**.
- **Marketing & Loyalty Data**: Retained for as long as your customer account remains active or until you request deletion.

#### 5. Your Rights as a Customer
Under applicable data protection laws (including the India DPDP Act 2023), you have the right to:
1. **Access**: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
2. **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate contact information.
3. **Withdrawal & Erasure**: Withdraw your consent for marketing communications or request anonymization of your loyalty profile.

#### 6. Contact & Grievance Redressal
If you have any questions or wish to exercise your data privacy rights, please contact our designated Grievance Officer:
- **Grievance Officer**: [Insert Name / Title, e.g. General Manager]
- **Email**: `privacy@[yourrestaurant].com`
- **Phone**: `+91-[Your Phone Number]`
- **Mailing Address**: [Insert Restaurant Address]
