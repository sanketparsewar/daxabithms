# DaxabitHMS: Terms of Service Requirements & Template

This document provides restaurant owners and operators with standard operational, legal, and commercial terms required when offering in-restaurant digital ordering, QR self-service, takeaway, and delivery through **DaxabitHMS**.

---

## 1. Key Operational Elements to Define

When deploying digital ordering (QR codes on tables, self-ordering kiosks, or delivery portals), restaurant owners must establish clear policies on:
1. **Order Acceptance & Modifiers**: Clarify that an order is only confirmed once accepted by the restaurant kitchen or cashier.
2. **Allergen Disclaimers**: Disclaimers regarding kitchen cross-contamination, especially in kitchens handling nuts, dairy, gluten, and shellfish.
3. **Cancellations & Refunds**: Policy regarding orders that have already entered the `PREPARING` cooking stage.
4. **Alcoholic Beverages**: Verification of legal drinking age upon delivery or table service.

---

## 2. Customer Terms of Service Template

*Below is a standard template for restaurant operators:*

---

### **TERMS OF SERVICE & DINING POLICIES**
**Last Updated: [Date]**
**Restaurant Name: [Insert Restaurant Name]**
**Legal Entity: [Insert Legal Company Name]**

#### 1. Scope & Acceptance
By placing an order through our digital QR menus, self-service kiosks, Point of Sale terminals, or direct delivery portals, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.

#### 2. Pricing & Taxes
- All prices displayed on our menus are in [Currency, e.g. INR / USD / EUR].
- In accordance with local tax laws, applicable statutory taxes (e.g., Goods and Services Tax / VAT / IVA) and any mandatory municipal fees are calculated and itemized on your final receipt.
- We make every effort to ensure accurate pricing; in the event of an inadvertent typographical error, we reserve the right to correct the charge prior to billing.

#### 3. Food Preparation & Allergen Notice
- **Allergies & Dietary Requirements**: While our kitchen takes extensive precautions to accommodate dietary requirements, our kitchen prepares items containing nuts, gluten, dairy, seafood, and soy. **Cross-contamination is possible**. Guests with severe allergies must notify a restaurant floor supervisor before ordering.
- **Modifiers**: Special cooking instructions (e.g. "less oil", "extra spicy") are fulfilled to the best of our culinary team's capability based on ingredient availability.

#### 4. Order Cancellations & Refunds
- **Dine-In & Takeaway**: Because food is prepared fresh to order, orders cannot be cancelled once cooking has commenced in the kitchen (`PREPARING` stage).
- **Quality Concerns**: If a dish does not meet quality standards or is served incorrectly, please notify the on-duty manager immediately. At management discretion, we will replace the dish or issue a refund or billing credit.
- **Refund Processing**: Cash refunds are issued immediately at the till. Card and UPI refunds are processed through the original payment channel within 3–7 business days depending on the customer's bank.

#### 5. Alcoholic Beverages
- Alcoholic beverages will strictly not be served or delivered to persons under the legal drinking age in our jurisdiction.
- Our staff reserves the right to request valid government-issued photographic identification (Passport, Driving License, Voter ID) prior to service.

#### 6. Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the restaurant's total aggregate liability arising out of any order or service shall be limited to the total monetary amount paid by the customer for that specific order.
